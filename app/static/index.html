GOT A QUESTION? WE'RE HERE TO HELP

In case you missed anything.

Everything you need to know about our fleet, autonomous IRROPS graph solver, and passenger rights.

What flight operations and charter services do you offer?

We provide scheduled airline disruption management, on-demand luxury private jet charters, and real-time autonomous re-accommodation. Our platform connects directly with Indian metro hubs (DEL, BOM, BLR, CCU, GOI) and international corridors with sub-millisecond graph re-ticketing, baggage re-routing, and guaranteed DGCA CAR Section 3 statutory compensation.

How does the autonomous disruption resolution process work?

Our engine continuously ingests telemetry and ATC ground stop data. When convective weather, monsoons, or mechanical delays strike, our constrained NetworkX graph engine computes Pareto-optimal alternative routings in under 1.1ms, automatically issuing digital boarding passes, meal vouchers, and direct-billing hotel suites with zero human intervention.

What airlines and aviation sectors are supported?

Our platform integrates with all leading Indian carriers (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, Vistara) as well as global Star Alliance and SkyTeam partner carriers. We support commercial scheduled passenger operations, luxury corporate private jets, and intermodal ground coach diversions.

Can I monitor live flights and disruption simulations?

Yes! Our interactive 3D WebGL Great-Circle Flight Radar showcases active sectors across the Indian subcontinent (DEL, BOM, BLR, CCU, GOI) and international corridors (JFK, LHR, DXB). You can also run live monsoon or squall line simulations to watch the autonomous solver in real time.

What is your pricing and statutory compensation structure?

We maintain one simple transparent fly rate with zero surge fees. When disruptions occur, passengers receive instant statutory compensation under DGCA CAR Section 3 (₹5,000 to ₹10,000), ₹450 airport meal vouchers, and automatic IATA Resolution 766 Op-Up cabin upgrades to Business Class J.

How fast is an affected passenger re-accommodated?

Re-accommodation is instantaneous. Traditional call center re-ticketing takes 24 minutes per passenger. Our algorithmic solver resolves multi-hub flight disruptions for hundreds of passengers simultaneously in less than 2 milliseconds.

What passenger welfare support is provided during ground stops?

We provide comprehensive passenger welfare including direct-billing hotel vouchers at contracted airport suites (Radisson Blu Aerocity / Taj Santacruz), luxury motor coach transfers to alternate hubs, complimentary lounge access, and 24/7 bilingual AI concierge assistance.

How are passengers notified during flight irregularities?

We believe multi-channel communication is key to a calm journey. We dispatch real-time flight updates, gate return notifications, and updated 2D barcode boarding passes via SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Wallet PKPass, and our interactive web concierge.
